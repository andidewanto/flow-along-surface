# Flow Along Surface

**Bend text and logos onto curved walls, or place copies along them.**

Free · Made on SketchUp 2026 for macOS · Version 1.2.0

[**Download**](https://andidewanto.github.io/flow-along-surface/) · [**Tutorial**](https://andidewanto.github.io/flow-along-surface/tutorial.html)

---

**Bend onto Surface:** 3D text, a logo or a panel follows a curved wall and keeps its length and thickness. Drag in the view to place it, then press Enter.

**Array Along Surface:** copies of an object placed along a curved wall by count or spacing, each turned with the curve.

Works on surfaces that bend one way: curved walls, S-shaped counters, round columns, arches and flat walls. Results are new groups, and one Undo removes them.

---

## Bahasa Indonesia

**Tekuk teks dan logo ke dinding lengkung, atau tempatkan copy di sepanjangnya.**

**Bend onto Surface:** teks 3D, logo, atau panel mengikuti dinding lengkung dengan panjang dan tebal tetap. Seret di viewport untuk menempatkannya, lalu tekan Enter.

**Array Along Surface:** copy object ditempatkan di sepanjang dinding lengkung berdasarkan jumlah atau jarak, masing-masing berputar mengikuti lengkungan.

Bekerja pada surface yang melengkung satu arah: dinding lengkung, counter bentuk S, kolom bulat, lengkungan plafon, dan dinding datar. Hasilnya group baru, dan satu kali Undo menghapusnya.
