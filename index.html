Flow Along Surface

Bend text and logos onto curved walls, or place copies along them.Tekuk teks dan logo ke dinding lengkung, atau tempatkan copy di sepanjangnya.

Free SketchUp extension · Made on SketchUp 2026 for macOS · VersionExtension SketchUp gratis · Dibuat di SketchUp 2026 untuk macOS · Versi 1.2.0

DownloadUnduh .rbzTutorial

Two tools for curved walls, counters and columns. Bend onto Surface wraps 3D text, a logo or a panel onto the surface and keeps its length, like Flow Along Surface in other 3D apps. Array Along Surface places copies of an object along the surface, each one turned to follow the curve.

Dua tool untuk dinding lengkung, counter, dan kolom. Bend onto Surface menekuk teks 3D, logo, atau panel ke surface dengan panjang tetap, seperti Flow Along Surface di aplikasi 3D lain. Array Along Surface menempatkan copy object di sepanjang surface, masing-masing berputar mengikuti lengkungan.

What it doesFungsinya

  • Bend onto Surface: 3D text, logos and panels follow the curve and keep their length and thickness. Letters with holes (A, d, o, e) stay solid.
  • Drag to place: drag in the view to slide the object along the surface and up or down, then press Enter.
  • Array Along Surface: copies by count or by spacing, with start and end offsets, height and gap, each copy turned with the curve.
  • Live preview: an orange outline shows the result before anything is made.
  • Nothing is changed: results are new groups, your original stays, and one Undo takes them away.
  • Works on: curved walls, S-shaped counters, round columns, arches and flat walls.
  • Bend onto Surface: teks 3D, logo, dan panel mengikuti lengkungan dengan panjang dan tebal tetap. Huruf berlubang (A, d, o, e) tetap solid.
  • Geser dengan mouse: seret di viewport untuk menggeser object sepanjang surface dan naik-turun, lalu tekan Enter.
  • Array Along Surface: copy berdasarkan jumlah atau jarak, dengan offset awal dan akhir, tinggi dan gap, tiap copy berputar mengikuti lengkungan.
  • Preview langsung: garis oranye menunjukkan hasilnya sebelum dibuat.
  • Tidak ada yang diubah: hasilnya group baru, object asli tetap ada, dan satu kali Undo membatalkannya.
  • Bekerja pada: dinding lengkung, counter bentuk S, kolom bulat, lengkungan plafon, dan dinding datar.

How to BendCara Bend

  1. Select the object you want to bend, such as 3D text or a logo (a group or component).
  2. Right-click and choose Flow Along Surface › Bend onto Surface.
  3. Click the target surface. It is outlined in blue, and an orange preview of the bent object appears.
  4. Click and drag in the view to move the object along the surface.
  5. When it looks right, click Bend (or press Enter).
  6. Done. The bent copy is created as a new group. Your original object stays untouched, unless you ticked Delete the original.
  1. Pilih object yang ingin ditekuk, misalnya teks 3D atau logo (group atau component).
  2. Klik kanan, lalu pilih Flow Along Surface › Bend onto Surface.
  3. Klik surface tujuan. Surface ditandai garis biru, dan preview oranye hasil tekukan langsung muncul.
  4. Klik dan seret di viewport untuk menggeser object di atas surface.
  5. Kalau posisinya sudah pas, klik Bend (atau tekan Enter).
  6. Selesai. Hasil tekukan dibuat sebagai group baru. Object asli tetap utuh, kecuali opsi Delete the original dicentang.

How to Array Along SurfaceCara Array Along Surface

  1. Select the object you want to repeat, such as a fence post, a lamp or a panel (a group or component).
  2. Right-click and choose Flow Along Surface › Array Along Surface.
  3. Click the target surface. It is outlined in blue, and an arrow shows the direction the copies will run.
  4. Set the copies in the dialog: choose Count (number of copies) or Spacing (distance between copies), then adjust the start/end offset, height and gap. Orange boxes preview where each copy will go.
  5. When it looks right, click Place.
  6. Done. All copies are placed in one new group and turn with the curve. Your original object stays untouched, unless you ticked Delete the original.
  1. Pilih object yang ingin diperbanyak, misalnya tiang pagar, lampu, atau panel (group atau component).
  2. Klik kanan, lalu pilih Flow Along Surface › Array Along Surface.
  3. Klik surface tujuan. Surface ditandai garis biru, dan tanda panah menunjukkan arah urutan copy.
  4. Atur copy di dialog: pilih Count (jumlah copy) atau Spacing (jarak antar copy), lalu sesuaikan offset awal/akhir, tinggi, dan gap. Kotak oranye menunjukkan posisi setiap copy.
  5. Kalau sudah pas, klik Place.
  6. Selesai. Semua copy masuk ke satu group baru dan berputar mengikuti lengkungan. Object asli tetap utuh, kecuali opsi Delete the original dicentang.
Tip Version 1 works on surfaces that bend one way only: curved walls, S-shaped counters, round columns, arches and flat walls. Domes, spheres and free-form surfaces are not supported yet, and the tool tells you so.Tips Versi 1 bekerja pada surface yang melengkung satu arah saja: dinding lengkung, counter bentuk S, kolom bulat, lengkungan plafon, dan dinding datar. Kubah, bola, dan bentuk bebas belum didukung, dan tool akan memberi tahu.

InstallInstalasi

  1. Download the .rbz above.
  2. SketchUp: Extensions › Extension Manager › Install Extension… and choose the .rbz.
  3. Use Extensions › Flow Along Surface, right-click a selected object, or show the toolbar in View › Toolbars. Full guide: Tutorial.
  1. Unduh file .rbz di atas.
  2. SketchUp: Extensions › Extension Manager › Install Extension… lalu pilih file .rbz.
  3. Gunakan Extensions › Flow Along Surface, klik kanan object yang dipilih, atau tampilkan toolbar di View › Toolbars. Panduan lengkap: Tutorial.

FAQ

"This surface bends in more than one direction.""This surface bends in more than one direction."

The surface is a dome, a sphere or a free form. Version 1 only works on surfaces that bend one way, such as curved walls, columns and arches.Surface-nya berupa kubah, bola, atau bentuk bebas. Versi 1 hanya bekerja pada surface yang melengkung satu arah, seperti dinding lengkung, kolom, dan lengkungan plafon.

"The edges of this surface branch…""The edges of this surface branch…"

Extra lines on the surface, such as ribs or drawn lines, make its curve unclear. Pick a clean surface, or remove the extra lines.Garis tambahan di surface, misalnya rib atau garis gambar, membuat lengkungannya tidak jelas. Pilih surface yang bersih, atau hapus garis tambahannya.

My text reads backwards after bending.Teks terbaca terbalik setelah ditekuk.

Undo, run Bend onto Surface again and tick Use the other side as front.Undo, jalankan Bend onto Surface lagi, lalu centang Use the other side as front.

My text runs up the wall instead of along it.Teks berjalan naik, bukan sepanjang dinding.

Tick or untick Turn 90°. The longest side of the object normally runs along the curve.Centang atau hapus centang Turn 90°. Sisi terpanjang object biasanya berjalan sepanjang lengkungan.

The bent object looks slightly faceted.Hasil tekukan terlihat sedikit bersegi.

The object follows the segments of your wall exactly. For a smoother bend, draw the wall's arc with more segments.Object mengikuti segmen dinding Anda dengan tepat. Untuk tekukan lebih halus, gambar arc dinding dengan segmen lebih banyak.

The array copies face the wrong way.Copy array menghadap ke arah yang salah.

The back of each copy (its green-axis side) sits on the surface. Use the Turn buttons, for example 180°.Bagian belakang tiap copy (sisi sumbu hijau) menempel di surface. Gunakan tombol Turn, misalnya 180°.

How do I type lengths?Bagaimana mengetik panjang?

Type the number with its unit, for example 20mm, 12,5mm or 1.2m. A dot or a comma both work. A bare number uses the unit of your model.Ketik angka beserta satuannya, misalnya 20mm, 12,5mm, atau 1.2m. Titik maupun koma sama-sama bisa. Angka tanpa satuan memakai satuan model Anda.

Is my original changed?Apakah object asli berubah?

No, unless you tick Delete the original. Undo brings it back.Tidak, kecuali Anda mencentang Delete the original. Undo mengembalikannya.
